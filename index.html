About the club

We are a group of students at the College of Engineering Kallooppara (CEK) who want to understand how systems break and how to keep them safe. We run workshops, play CTFs together and share what we learn here.

New to security? Start with the posts tagged beginner. You do not need any experience to join.

Everything on this blog is for education. Only test systems you own or have written permission to test.
